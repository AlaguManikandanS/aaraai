# Aaraai

AI-powered research paper understanding and analysis platform using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

## Vision

Aaraai helps students, researchers, and readers understand research papers by allowing them to upload papers, ask questions, explore concepts, and receive grounded answers with references to the original document.

## Problem

Research papers are often difficult for students and beginners to understand because of technical terminology, complex methodologies, mathematical equations, and large amounts of information.

## Solution

Aaraai is an AI-powered research paper understanding platform that allows users to upload research papers, ask questions about their content, and receive grounded answers with references to the original document.

## Core Features

- Research paper PDF upload
- Automatic document processing
- Paper overview and summarization
- Question answering using RAG
- Source/page citations
- Interactive paper chat
